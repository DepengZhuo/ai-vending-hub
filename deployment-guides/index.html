Deployment Guides

How to deploy AI vending in each venue type, including how to choose products, plan the rollout, and measure success.

9 venue guides · Step-by-step

Apartment Buildings

Residents shop daily for essentials and late-night cravings, so an unused lobby corner becomes 24/7 convenience retail.

Factories

Shift workers need food, drinks, and safety essentials around the clock — beyond what cafeterias serve.

Gyms

Loyal members and predictable pre- and post-workout demand make gyms strong AI vending locations.

Hospitals

Hospitals run 24/7 with staff on shift rotations and visitors with unpredictable schedules. Unattended retail fills gaps without adding labor.

Hotels

24/7 guest convenience without staffing a shop. Lobbies, gyms, and pool areas are natural spots, but the product mix must fit guest psychology.

Offices

Break-room convenience that cuts queues, supports wellness, and improves satisfaction.

Schools & Campuses

Students are on campus before, between, and after meal times. AI vending captures every gap between structured meals.

Airports

Very high traffic, time pressure, and security-controlled spaces with predictable dwell-time demand.

Construction Sites

Remote sites with short breaks and no nearby retail — fuel, hydration, and safety gear on site 24/7.
