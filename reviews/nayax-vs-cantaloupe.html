Nayax vs. Cantaloupe: Payment & Telemetry Platforms Compared

Updated August 2026 · Editorial Team · 7 min read

Nayax and Cantaloupe are the two big payment and telemetry platforms for unattended retail. Both work well; the fit depends on hardware, fees, and region.

Five-dimension comparison

DimensionNayaxCantaloupe
PaymentFull cashless suite: NFC, QR, wallets, readersCashless plus cash-handling ecosystem
InventoryReal-time telemetry, remote machine dataTelemetry via Seed sensors and devices
Product selectionCatalog and pricing managementCatalog management across machines
AnalyticsOperator dashboards, alertsEnterprise reporting suite
Loss preventionCashless audit trail, cash accountabilityCash accountability, cashless integration

Bottom line first

Nayax and Cantaloupe both provide card readers, telemetry, and dashboards for vending and micro markets. Choose based on hardware compatibility, fee structure, and the software your team prefers.

At a glance

FactorNayaxCantaloupe
Core businessPayment and telemetryPayment and telemetry
FootprintGlobalStrong in North America
HardwareReaders, cashless devicesReaders, seed devices
PlatformCloud dashboard and appCloud dashboard and app
Micro marketsSupportedSupported

What they do

Both platforms turn any vending machine into a connected device: card payments, remote monitoring, cashless transactions, and reporting. Neither makes the vending machine itself.

Fees to compare

FeeTypical range
Transaction processing2 to 5 percent
Reader hardwareOne-time or lease
Monthly platform$5 to $15 per device

Where each fits

  • Choose Nayax for global deployments and broad hardware compatibility.
  • Choose Cantaloupe for deep North American vending integrations and micro-market ecosystems.

Integration questions

  • Does the platform integrate with your vending management software?
  • Can you switch processors without changing readers?
  • What happens to devices if you change platforms?

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a payment platform for AI vending?

Yes. AI vending requires electronic payment, and the platform also provides the telemetry and dashboard.

Can I switch platforms later?

Usually yes, but readers may need replacement and contracts may have terms. Check before committing.

Which has lower fees?

It depends on volume, card mix, and negotiated rates. Request quotes on your projected volume.
