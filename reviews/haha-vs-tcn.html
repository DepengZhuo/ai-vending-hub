Haha vs. TCN: Two Routes to AI Vending

Updated August 2026 · Editorial Team · 7 min read

Haha and TCN both sell vision-based AI vending hardware but approach the market differently. Compare them on products, tech, and service before choosing.

Bottom line first

Both companies sell AI vending machines with computer-vision recognition, cashless payment, and cloud management. The practical differences are product range, customization, and regional support.

Products and technology

Haha positions itself around AI vending and smart coolers with vision recognition and remote management. TCN sells a wider catalog of vending machines, including AI smart fridges and coolers alongside traditional models.

Both use on-device cameras and cloud dashboards, so the core shopping experience is similar: open, grab, close, and pay.

Where each wins

  • Choose Haha if you want a dedicated AI-vending product line and strong presence in Western markets.
  • Choose TCN if you need a broad hardware catalog, including traditional machines, from one supplier.

What to verify before buying

  • Ask for accuracy results tested on your product mix.
  • Confirm total cost: hardware, shipping, customs, software, and payment fees.
  • Check service and spare-parts coverage in your country.
  • Request a pilot unit before fleet purchasing.

Frequently asked questions

Are Haha and TCN machines compatible with any payment processor?

Most support common cashless readers and platforms, but confirm compatibility with your payment provider before ordering.

Which company is better for a US deployment?

Both serve the US market. Compare local service response times and spare-parts availability, not just brochure specs.

Can I test recognition with my own products?

Both vendors typically support pilot programs. Always test with your real SKUs and packaging.
