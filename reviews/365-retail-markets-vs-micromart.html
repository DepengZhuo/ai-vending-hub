365 Retail Markets vs. Micromart: Micro-Market Platforms Compared

Updated August 2026 · Editorial Team · 6 min read

365 Retail Markets powers kiosk-led micro markets; Micromart builds autonomous smart stores with computer vision. Compare platform breadth, hardware, and fit before choosing.

Bottom line first

365 Retail Markets is the incumbent micro-market platform: self-checkout kiosks, coolers and AVS hardware, and a back-office suite for breakrooms and cafeterias. Micromart sells self-contained autonomous smart stores — glass-door coolers with computer vision that turn a lobby or office into a 24/7 shop. Choose 365 for kiosk-led markets with wide assortments; choose Micromart for a compact, plug-and-play smart store.

What they do

Both run unattended retail, but the models differ. 365 Retail Markets is a platform company: operators install kiosks and coolers in an existing space and manage inventory, payments, and loyalty from one suite. Micromart is a product company: it builds the smart-cooler unit and sells a complete package — hardware, software, and payments — so a single unit becomes a store without a kiosk.

Where each fits

  • Choose 365 Retail Markets for existing breakrooms or cafeterias with room for a kiosk-led micro market and a wide product assortment.
  • Choose Micromart for compact lobbies and offices where a full market footprint does not fit, and you want grab-and-go checkout without a kiosk.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between a micro market and a smart store?

A micro market is a kiosk-checkout mini-store set up in an existing space; a smart store is a self-contained unit that identifies items and charges automatically, so no checkout kiosk is needed.

Can Micromart scale like a 365 micro market?

Micromart units work well as compact single-location stores. For wide assortments and multiple coolers in one space, a kiosk-led micro market scales differently.

Which platform is cheaper?

Pricing depends on location, hardware, and volume. Request quotes on your projected deployment size.
