Maintenance & SLA Guide for Smart Vending

AI vending has fewer mechanical jams than traditional machines, but tech issues need a clear service plan. This guide covers SLAs and maintenance routines.

Quick answer

Plan for preventive checks, a defined response-time SLA, and a spare parts stock. Modern units need less mechanical service but faster tech support.

What actually breaks

ComponentTypical failure
ConnectivityNetwork dropouts
Payment readerCard read issues
CoolingTemperature drift
CamerasDust and angle shifts

SLA basics

  • Define response and resolution times in the contract.
  • Include remote diagnosis before dispatching a technician.
  • Agree on spare parts stock and dispatch coverage area.

Preventive maintenance

  1. Clean camera lenses and check sensor angles quarterly.
  2. Review temperature logs for refrigerated units weekly.
  3. Check payment reader firmware and connectivity monthly.
  4. Test exception handling and refund workflows monthly.

Reducing downtime

  • Use telemetry alerts to spot issues before customers do.
  • Keep a small parts kit for common failures.
  • Choose vendors with regional service coverage.

Frequently asked questions

Do AI vending machines need a lot of maintenance?

Less mechanical maintenance than coil machines, but more software and connectivity support.

What SLA should I ask for?

A common target is next-business-day response with remote diagnosis first.

Who handles maintenance?

Vendors, local service partners, or your own staff, depending on fleet size.
