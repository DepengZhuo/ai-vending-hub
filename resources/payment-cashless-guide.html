Payment & Cashless Guide for Smart Vending

Cashless payment is table stakes for AI vending. This guide covers reader hardware, fee structures, and how to pick a provider.

Quick answer

Choose a payment platform that integrates with your machine and telemetry, supports cards and wallets, and has fees you can model at your sales volume.

Payment options

MethodNotes
Contactless cardsStandard in North America
Mobile walletsApple Pay, Google Pay
QR and app payCommon with specific platforms
CashOptional, adds hardware

Fee structure

  • Per-transaction percentage (typically 2 to 5 percent).
  • Monthly platform or device fees.
  • Interchange differences by card type.

Choosing a provider

  • Confirm compatibility with your machine model.
  • Compare dashboards: exception handling and refunds matter.
  • Check settlement speed and reporting quality.
  • Ask about bundling payment with telemetry.

Common pitfalls

  • Choosing readers that lock you into one processor.
  • Ignoring offline payment fallback rules.
  • Forgetting PCI and data security requirements.

Payment method fees at a glance

Fees vary by provider and volume, but the ranges are consistent enough to budget against:

Frequently asked questions

Do AI vending machines need cash?

No. Most deployments are cashless-only, which simplifies hardware and lifts basket size.

Which provider should I use?

Nayax and Cantaloupe are the major platforms; compare fees and integrations at your volume.

What fees should I budget?

Plan 2 to 5 percent of sales plus a monthly platform fee per device.
