How to use this calculator

  1. Set the number of machines and expected transactions per machine per day.
  2. Enter average order value and gross margin.
  3. Add monthly operating costs per machine.
  4. Compare the payback period against your target.

What the numbers mean

  • Gross profit is revenue times margin, before operating costs.
  • Net profit subtracts monthly costs per machine.
  • Payback is the device price divided by monthly net profit.

Reality checks

Estimates assume stable traffic and no major downtime. Real results depend on location, spoilage, theft, and restocking efficiency. Run a pilot before trusting the model.

Frequently asked questions

What is a realistic transactions-per-day number?

10 to 40 is common for good locations; strong gyms and offices can exceed that.

What margin should I use?

40 to 60 percent gross margin is typical for vending, before spoilage and shrinkage.

How accurate is this calculator?

It is a planning tool, not a quote. Use it to compare scenarios and validate with a pilot.
