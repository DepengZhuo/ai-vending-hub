About AI Vending Hub

AI Vending Hub is an independent media site for the smart retail and AI vending industry. We publish guides, comparisons, and market intelligence for operators, investors, and venue owners.

Independent

We don't sell equipment. Coverage is built to help you compare, not to favor any vendor.

Verifiable

We link sources where we can and mark data with check dates so you know how fresh it is.

Plain English

No jargon walls. If a concept needs a diagram or a table, we use one.

What we cover

  • Market reports and industry analysis.
  • Knowledge Base explainers: AI vending, smart retail, computer vision, grab-and-go, smart coolers.
  • Neutral comparisons and vendor reviews built on fixed criteria.
  • A company directory covering the vendors building this space.
  • Tools & resources: buyer's checklists, cost breakdowns, location SOPs, and an ROI calculator.
  • Deployment guides for nine venue types, from apartments to construction sites.
