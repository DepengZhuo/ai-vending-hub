Grabot

AI smart coolers with on-device computer vision

Est. China · Global (China-based, exhibited at CAMA)

Visit Website

Overview

Grabot is an AI-powered unattended retail technology company specializing in intelligent hardware, computer vision, secure payment, and cloud management. At CAMA and NAMA shows, it has presented flagship AI smart coolers that run edge AI on-device, meaning recognition works without cloud dependency.

Market position
