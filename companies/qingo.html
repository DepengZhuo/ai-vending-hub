QINGO (Wuhan Qinggouyun Technology)

Smart self-serve cabinets with RFID and vision

Est. 2017 · China (Wuhan), expanding internationally

Visit Website

Overview

QINGO is the AI vending brand of Wuhan Qinggouyun Technology, a Chinese developer of smart self-serve cabinets. The company builds on RFID and vision technology to offer scan-to-open cabinets with automatic item recognition and cashless payment. Its products have been shown at China's NAMA show, and the brand is positioning itself for international expansion.

Products & technology

  • QINGO AI vending cabinets: scan-to-open smart cabinets with automatic item recognition.
  • HGOBOX smart cabinets: RFID and vision-based units for unattended retail.
  • Cloud management: sales data, inventory, and user analytics for operators.

Technology route: RFID tags, antennas, machine vision, and deep learning in smart vending cabinets; QR scan-to-open with Alipay and WeChat Pay

Market position

QINGO competes on low-cost, high-volume cabinet hardware from China's supply chain, targeting operators who want entry-level AI vending with proven RFID-vision technology.

Strengths

  • RFID plus vision hybrid gives reliable attribution in controlled settings.
  • Deep experience with Alipay and WeChat Pay integrations.
  • Cost-effective manufacturing base in China.
