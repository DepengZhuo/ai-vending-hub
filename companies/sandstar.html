SandStar

AI vending machines and AI store solutions with edge computer vision

Founded 2015 · Global (HQ China)

Visit Website

Overview

SandStar is a global AI retail technology company providing AI vending machines, AI store solutions, and analytics. Its vision models are trained to recognize a very large SKU library, and the company emphasizes low-cost, high-performance edge AI that works without depending on cloud latency.

Company profile at a glance

DimensionSandStar

Best-fit venuesVending and full-store retail; venues needing low-latency, offline-capable recognition
Market focusGlobal AI retail pioneer; edge AI and large-scale SKU recognition

Products & technology

  • VRK AI vending kiosk: a computer-vision kiosk designed as an elegant unattended storefront.
  • AI vending machines and smart coolers: open-door vision units for fresh and packaged goods.
  • AI store solutions: full-store computer vision for checkout-free and analytics use cases.

Technology route: Computer vision and semantic action recognition running on edge hardware such as NVIDIA Jetson; SKU recognition scale and AI store analytics

Market position

SandStar positions itself as a global pioneer in AI retail, serving both vending machines and full stores, with strong R&D in edge AI and large-scale SKU recognition.

Strengths

  • Edge AI reduces connectivity dependency and operating cost.
  • Very large trained SKU library with high claimed accuracy.
  • Covers both vending and full-store smart retail formats.

Questions to ask before you buy

  • What accuracy do you report on my product categories and packaging?
  • How does the edge AI handle model updates in the field?
  • What is the deployment model for North American operators?
  • What are hardware and software costs at my volume?
