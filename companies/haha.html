Haha Vending

AI smart vending machines and smart fridges for global unattended retail

Founded 2017 · Global (HQ China; US, UK, Europe, Singapore)

Visit Website

Overview

Haha Vending is a global provider of AI-powered smart vending machines, founded in 2017. It integrates AI vision, hardware, software, payments, and remote management into one solution for unattended retail. The company operates subsidiaries in the US, UK, Europe, China, Hong Kong, and Singapore, and reports an installed base of more than 150,000 devices and over 80 patents.

Products & technology

  • AI smart combo machines: snack and drink units with computer-vision recognition and cashless payment.
  • AI smart fridges and smart coolers: open-door vision units for fresh food, drinks, and multi-item baskets.
  • Refrigerated beverage machines: high-capacity units (roughly 250 to 800 bottles) with app management.
  • Cloud and app platform: 4G or Wi-Fi remote management, sales reporting, pricing control, and SDK options for custom integrations.

Technology route: Deep-learning computer vision for product recognition, app-based remote management, cashless payment, and SDK integrations

Market position

Haha positions itself as a full-stack AI retail provider with a strong presence in Western markets. It offers US local stock, custom graphics and logo options, and NAMA membership, targeting operators who want vision-enabled machines with local support.

Strengths

  • Full stack: vision, hardware, software, payments, and remote management.
  • Global footprint with subsidiaries in the US, UK, Europe, and Singapore.
  • Company-reported scale: 150,000+ devices, 80+ patents, and 20,000+ customers (verify current figures).
  • Customization and SDK support for branded deployments.

Questions to ask before you buy

  • What recognition accuracy do you guarantee on my product mix?
  • What certifications does each model carry for my market?
  • How is US and European service, spare parts, and technical support handled?
  • What is the total cost over 36 months, including software and payment fees?
  • Can I run a pilot unit before committing to a fleet?
