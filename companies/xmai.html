XMAI Vending

AI vending machines and smart coolers with cloud management

Brand under Midea Group ecosystem · Global (China-based, expanding in North America)

Visit Website

Overview

XMAI Vending is an AI-powered automated retail provider developing intelligent vending machines, smart vending machines, and smart coolers. The brand operates under the Midea Group ecosystem, which gives it manufacturing scale and supply-chain depth. In 2026, XMAI launched a US distributor program to help operators and entrepreneurs deploy its AI-enabled units.

Products & technology

  • AI vending machines: vision-enabled units with cashless payment and remote management.
  • Smart coolers: open-door vision coolers for fresh food and drinks.
  • Cloud management platform: remote monitoring, pricing, and inventory tools.

Technology route: Computer-vision item recognition, digital payments, cloud management, and remote monitoring across AI vending machines and smart coolers

Market position

XMAI targets operators and distributors in North America with a partner program, leveraging Midea's manufacturing background to compete on price and scale.

Strengths

  • Manufacturing scale through the Midea Group ecosystem.
  • Combined AI, payment, and remote-monitoring stack.
  • Active push into the US market through distribution partners.
